================================================================================
                    SIMPLE IMPROVING TRAITS - COMPLETE GUIDE
================================================================================

This guide explains how to improve each trait, including stat limits, required
actions, base values, and scaling increments.

Every positive trait can be obtained; every negative trait can be removed.

Progression traits earn a percentage bonus that grows the more you use them:
- mining, melee, ranged, walking, hunger, armor
- mender, pilferer, resourceful, forager, furtive, precise

Unlock traits are one-time unlocks with no percentage to grind:
- clothier, technical, hardyhealth
- bowyer, improviser, tinkerer, merciless

Negative traits from your starting class can be removed by leveling the matching
positive trait. See "Negative Traits - How To Remove Them" below.

Use '/trait [name]' to see more info about leveling it up and your progress so
far in-game. You can also read this (mostly complete) document.

================================================================================
                              MINING - HARDY TRAIT
================================================================================

Max Bonus: 50% mining speed
Base Increment: 100 points for first 1%
Scaling: Each additional 1% requires +100 more points (100, 200, 300, 400...)

HOW TO IMPROVE:
- Mine stone blocks: 1 point each
- Mine ore blocks: 5 points each (configurable via OreMultiplier)

SPECIAL MECHANICS:
- Per-pickaxe tracking: Each pickaxe type tracks progress independently
- Encourages using many different pickaxe types
- Only blocks broken with pickaxes count

EXAMPLE PROGRESSION:
- Mine 100 stone with copper pickaxe -> +1% (copper now needs 200 for next)
- Mine 200 more with copper pickaxe -> +1% (copper now needs 300 for next)
- Switch to tin-bronze pickaxe
- Mine 100 stone with tin-bronze -> +1% (tin-bronze now needs 200 for next)

================================================================================
                            MELEE DAMAGE - SOLDIER TRAIT
================================================================================

Max Bonus: 50% melee damage
Base Increment: 100 damage for first 1%
Scaling: Each additional 1% requires +100 more damage (100, 200, 300, 400...)

HOW TO IMPROVE:
- Deal damage with qualifying melee weapons

QUALIFYING WEAPONS:
- Swords (code starts with sword-, blade-, longsword-, shortsword-)
- Falx (code starts with falx-)
- Spears (code starts with spear-)

SPECIAL MECHANICS:
- Per-weapon tracking: Each specific weapon tracks progress independently
- Encourages crafting and using many different weapon types

VANILLA INTERACTION:
- Vanilla Soldier gives +30% melee damage
- Blackguard players can earn up to 20% more (to reach 50% cap)
- Commoners can earn the full 50%

================================================================================
                           RANGED - FOCUSED TRAIT
================================================================================

Max Damage Bonus: 50%
Max Accuracy Bonus: 50%
Max Distance Bonus: 50%
Base Increment: 100 damage for first 1%
Scaling: Each additional 1% requires +100 more damage (100, 200, 300, 400...)

HOW TO IMPROVE:
- Deal ranged damage with projectiles

QUALIFYING WEAPONS:
- Arrows (projectile code starts with arrow- or is arrow)
- Sling stones (projectile code starts with stone- or is stone)
- Thrown spears (projectile code starts with spear- or thrownspear)

SPECIAL MECHANICS:
- Per-weapon-combo tracking: Each bow+arrow combination tracks separately
- Melee hits with a bow do NOT count
- Only projectile damage counts

EXAMPLE PROGRESSION:
- 100 damage with crude bow + flint arrows -> +1% all stats
- 200 more with crude bow + flint arrows -> +1% all stats
- Switch to crude bow + copper arrows
- 100 damage with new combo -> +1% all stats (new weapon combo)

VANILLA INTERACTION:
- Vanilla Focused gives +20% damage, +30% accuracy, +20% distance
- Hunters can earn: +30% more damage, +20% more accuracy, +30% more distance
- Commoners can earn the full 50% for all stats

================================================================================
                        WALK SPEED - FLEETFOOTED TRAIT
================================================================================

Max Bonus: 15% walk speed (115% total)
Base Increment: 1000 blocks walked for first 1%
Scaling: Each additional 1% requires +1000 more blocks (1000, 2000, 3000...)

HOW TO IMPROVE:
- Walk around (horizontal distance only)

SPECIAL MECHANICS:
- Only horizontal movement counts (X/Z axis)
- Climbing and falling do NOT count (no ladder/falling exploits)
- Teleportation ignored (distances >10 blocks per tick discarded)
- Tracked every 500ms

VANILLA INTERACTION:
- Vanilla Fleetfooted gives +10% walk speed
- Hunter/Clockmaker can earn up to 5% more (to reach 15% cap)
- Commoners can earn the full 15%

================================================================================
                              HUNGER RATE
================================================================================

Target Hunger Rate: 75% (configurable)
Base Increment: 300 seconds (5 minutes) at full saturation for first 1%
Scaling: Each additional 1% requires +60 more seconds (5 min, 6 min, 7 min...)

HOW TO IMPROVE:
- Stay at maximum saturation (1500/1500)

MAX CREDITS BY CLASS:
- Non-Ravenous players (100% base): Need 25 credits to reach 75%
- Ravenous players (130% base, Blackguard): Need 55 credits to reach 75%

SPECIAL MECHANICS:
- Only time at EXACTLY max saturation counts
- Tracked every 1 second
- All classes can reach the same 75% target

================================================================================
                    ARMOR PROGRESSION - SOLDIER TRAIT
================================================================================

ARMOR DURABILITY:
Max Bonus: 50%

WALK SPEED PENALTY REDUCTION:
Max Bonus: 50%

--- XP SOURCE 1: FIRST-TIME EQUIP BONUS ---

Equipping new armor grants immediate bonuses to BOTH durability AND walk speed:
- Light armor (leather, gambeson, jerkin, improvised): +1% each
- Chain armor (chain, lamellar): +1% each
- Brigandine: +2% each
- Scale armor: +3% each
- Plate armor: +3% each

Each unique armor piece only grants this bonus ONCE per player.

--- XP SOURCE 2: DAMAGE BLOCKED (Durability Only) ---

Base Increment: 100 damage blocked for first credit
Scaling: Each additional credit requires +100 more damage
Per-piece tracking: Each armor piece tracks damage independently

Damage distribution by hit probability:
- Head: 20%
- Body: 50%
- Legs: 30%

--- XP SOURCE 3: ARMOR REPAIRS (Durability Only) ---

Base Increment: 1 repair for first credit
Scaling: Each additional credit requires +1 more repair (1, 2, 3...)
Per-piece tracking: Each armor piece tracks repairs independently

VANILLA INTERACTION:
- Vanilla Soldier gives +15% durability and -25% speed penalty
- Blackguard can earn: +35% more durability, +25% more speed reduction
- Commoners can earn the full 50% for both

================================================================================
                  NEGATIVE TRAITS - HOW TO REMOVE THEM
================================================================================

Several starting classes come with a negative trait. NONE of them are stuck with
it. Every negative can be worn away by leveling the matching positive trait. This
is not Hunter-specific: Tailor, Hunter, Malefactor, Clockmaker, and Blackguard
all have negatives, and they all come off the same way. A Blackguard's
Nearsighted (-15% ranged damage with bows) is removed exactly like a Tailor's
Civil or a Hunter's Claustrophobic.

HOW REMOVAL WORKS:
1. Each negative trait is tied to one positive trait.
2. Every level you earn in that positive trait removes 1 point of the penalty.
   A -15% penalty is fully gone after 15 levels of the matching positive trait.
3. The penalty shrinks in your character sheet as you level. When it reaches 0,
   the negative trait disappears from the display.
4. Only levels earned PAST the penalty start building a positive bonus. Until the
   negative is paid off, you are climbing back to zero, not above it.
5. A penalised class can earn EXTRA levels beyond the normal cap, so that after
   clearing the penalty it can still reach the full positive bonus. Example: a
   Blackguard clears -15% Nearsighted over 15 ranged levels, then earns the full
   +50% on top, for 65 ranged levels in total.
6. Some negatives cover two things at once (Weak, Frail, Kind, Heavyhanded,
   Claustrophobic). Each part has its own threshold. The health penalties on Weak
   (-2 HP) and Frail (-2.5 HP) are restored automatically when their paired
   percentage penalty is cleared.

REMOVAL TABLE:

Negative Trait | Class       | Penalty                  | Level This Positive | To
---------------|-------------|--------------------------|---------------------|----
Civil          | Tailor      | -10% foraging loot       | Forager             | 10
Weak           | Tailor      | -2 HP and -10% mining    | Hardy (mining)      | 10
Kind           | Tailor      | -10% animal loot         | Resourceful         | 10
Kind           | Tailor      | -25% harvest speed       | Resourceful         | 25
Farsighted     | Hunter      | -15% melee damage        | Soldier (melee)     | 15
Claustrophobic | Hunter      | -15% ore and -10% mining | Hardy (mining)      | 10
Nervous        | Malef/Clock | -15% melee damage        | Soldier (melee)     | 15
Frail          | Malef/Clock | -2.5 HP and -25% range   | Focused (ranged)    | 25
Nearsighted    | Blackguard  | -15% ranged damage       | Focused (ranged)    | 15
Heavyhanded    | Blackguard  | -10% cracked vessel loot | Pilferer            | 10
Heavyhanded    | Blackguard  | -15% foraging loot       | Forager             | 15
Heavyhanded    | Blackguard  | -20% wild crop drop      | Forager             | 20
Ravenous       | Blackguard  | +30% hunger rate         | Hunger              | 30

("Malef/Clock" means the trait appears on the Malefactor and/or Clockmaker.)

EXAMPLE (Blackguard removing Nearsighted, then earning the bonus):
- Start:            "Nearsighted (-15% ranged damage)"
- Focused level 6:  "Nearsighted (-9% ranged damage)"
- Focused level 15: Nearsighted removed from display
- Focused level 25: "Focused (+10% ranged damage)"
- Focused level 65: "Focused (+50% ranged damage)" (the full cap)

HUNTER BONUS - CLAUSTROPHOBIC OVERCOME:
The Hunter's Claustrophobic penalty is gone by mining level 10, like any other
negative. As an extra milestone, at mining level 100 the Hunter earns a cosmetic
"Claustrophobic Overcome" badge on the character sheet. The penalty is long gone
before that point; the badge is just recognition for going the distance.

================================================================================
                 COMBAT OVERHAUL COMPATIBILITY (APPENDIX)
================================================================================

When the Combat Overhaul mod (or its fork) is installed, Seraph Leveling feeds
its bonuses into Combat Overhaul's own combat stats. This is turned on
automatically and can be disabled with EnableCombatOverhaulCompat in the config.

--- WEAPON PROFICIENCIES ---

Each weapon category has its own proficiency that you raise by dealing damage
with that kind of weapon. The curve matches the melee and ranged traits: 100
damage for the first 1%, then +100 more per 1%, tracked per weapon.

Proficiency          | Max Bonus | Raised By
---------------------|-----------|--------------------------------
Bows                 | +50%      | Dealing damage with bows
Crossbows            | +50%      | Dealing damage with crossbows
Firearms             | +50%      | Dealing damage with firearms
Slings               | +30%      | Dealing damage with slings
One-Handed Swords    | +30%      | One-handed sword melee
Two-Handed Swords    | +30%      | Two-handed sword melee
Spears               | +30%      | Spear melee
Javelins             | +30%      | Javelin throws
Maces                | +30%      | Mace melee
Clubs                | +30%      | Club melee
Halberds             | +30%      | Halberd melee
Poleaxes*            | +30%      | Poleaxe melee
Axes                 | +30%      | Combat axe melee
Quarterstaff         | +30%      | Quarterstaff melee
Steady Aim           | +50%      | Any ranged damage (accuracy)

* Poleaxes are a separate proficiency only in the Combat Overhaul fork.

--- DAMAGE TIERS (ARMOR PENETRATION) ---

Your ranged and melee leveling also raises Combat Overhaul's damage-tier
bonuses, which improve how well your attacks punch through armor.

--- BODY DAMAGE FACTORS ---

Some Combat Overhaul classes take extra damage to certain body parts, or come
with a perk. Seraph lets you earn these down or up:
- Big Head / Thick Skull: head and face damage taken (Clockmaker starts at +50%)
- Leg Day: leg and feet damage taken (Blackguard starts at +100%, plus +25% jump)

--- BUYING BACK NEGATIVE COMBAT TRAITS ---

Combat Overhaul classes that start with a combat penalty can earn it back, the
same idea as the vanilla negative traits above:
- Clumsy Hands / Weak Hand: reduced weapon proficiency
- Trembling Aim: reduced steady aim
- Nervous / Frightened / Fear of Melee: reduced melee damage tier

Class-granted proficiencies that are perks are kept, and your earned bonus stacks
on top of them.

================================================================================
                                SLEEP BUFF
================================================================================

Sleeping in a bed can grant a temporary XP multiplier that speeds up ALL trait
gains. This is DISABLED by default; a server admin turns it on in the config.

MULTIPLIERS:
- Linen and old beds: 2x XP
- Hay beds: 1.5x XP

HOW IT WORKS:
- Sleep in a bed to receive the buff
- The buff lasts 1 in-game day by default (SleepBuffDurationDays)
- It applies to every trait's XP, including sneaking distance for Furtive
- It does NOT persist across server restarts; only in-game time matters

CONFIGURATION (in ModConfig/SeraphLeveling.json):
- EnableSleepBuff (default false)
- SleepBuffLinenBedMultiplier (default 2.0)
- SleepBuffHayBedMultiplier (default 1.5)
- SleepBuffDurationDays (default 1.0)

Use '/trait sleepbuff' to see your current buff status.
